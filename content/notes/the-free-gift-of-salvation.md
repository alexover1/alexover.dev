Scripture references on how salvation is given to us as a free gift by the grace of God through Jesus.

<!--more-->

### {{< verse "John 3:16" >}}
- Anyone who believes will have eternal life

### {{< verse "Ephesians 2:8-9" >}}
- We are saved by grace and through faith

### {{< verse "Acts 4:12" >}}
- Jesus is the only name that saves

### {{< verse "2 Corinthians 5:21" >}}
- Our sin was placed on Jesus
- We are attributed his righteousness (right standing with God)

### {{< verse "John 14:6-7" >}}
- Jesus is how we know God

### {{< verse "Romans 5:8" >}}
- He did not wait for us to become clean to save us, but died for us while we were still sinners

### {{< verse "Titus 3:5" >}}
- The Holy Spirit performs the inner transformation, changing our hearts and our lives (John 3:5)

### {{< verse "John 1:12" >}}
- We have a new, intimate relationship now as we are adopted into sonship
